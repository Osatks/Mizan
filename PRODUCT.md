# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Muslims anywhere in the world, reading in any of the site's 15 languages (Arabic, English, French, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, Malay, Persian, Bengali, Russian, German, Spanish, Ukrainian, Uzbek, Kazakh). All 15 languages are first-class (confirmed 2026-11-08); three are right-to-left (Arabic, Urdu, Persian). Typical visit: a quick, practical question asked on a phone: how much zakat do I owe, how is this estate divided, when is the next prayer, which way is the qibla, what is today's Hijri date.

## Product Purpose

Mizan (ميزان, "the balance") is a free, accurate set of Islamic tools on the web, together with the Mizan phone app (Google Play and App Store). The website has two equal jobs (confirmed 2026-11-08): let visitors use the tools directly, and bring them to the app, which adds the Mushaf with tafsir and recitation, 334 sourced adhkar, prayer alerts and widgets. Success: a visitor gets a correct, explained answer quickly, and installs the app.

## Positioning

Shari'ah calculations built on the position of the majority of jurists, with the evidence and points of disagreement shown next to every result. Everything is computed on the visitor's device: no account, no data sent to a server, works offline (PWA). Free, in 15 languages.

## Operating Context

- Static site generated by `build.py` (Python, no dependencies) from `src/templates/base.html`, `src/pages/*`, `src/content/*`, `src/assets/*`, and `src/i18n/*`; output in `dist/`, served from Cloudflare.
- Each language is built from the same templates; text goes through `{{t:...}}` keys, so layout must hold for long German/Russian strings and for RTL scripts.
- Installable PWA (manifest, service worker), offline support.
- Google AdSense is configured (`config.json`) but its ad slots are not a binding design element.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Tools (10): zakat, zakat al-fitr, inheritance (fara'id), Hijri converter, age calculator, prayer times, qibla compass, tasbih counter, Islamic events countdown, fidya and kaffarah.
- Arabic-only articles (6), plus about, contact, privacy, terms, disclaimer, app, and 404 pages.
- Results are guidance; the site always says to consult scholars on specific cases.
- Prayer times via astronomical formulas with a chosen calculation method; Hijri dates use the Umm al-Qura calendar.

- Logo: a golden scale on deep emerald with a faint eight-point star (`branding/icon/`, `src/assets/img/favicon.svg`), shared with the iOS and Android apps. Must be kept (confirmed 2026-11-08).
- Colours, the accent-colour picker, and the overall look are not binding and may be replaced (confirmed 2026-11-08).

## Product Principles

1. Correct first: every result comes with its rule and evidence; never trade accuracy for speed or looks.
2. The answer in one glance: the result a visitor came for is the most visible thing on the page.
3. Equal in every language: no language is a degraded translation of another, and RTL is never an afterthought.
4. Private and offline by default: nothing that needs a server, an account, or tracking to work.
5. Respectful: sacred text and the Shari'ah subject are treated with dignity, never as decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many visitors are on low-end phones and slow connections. Text must stay readable in Arabic, Persian, Urdu (Nastaliq), Bengali, and Cyrillic scripts. Must respect reduced-motion and system light/dark preferences.
