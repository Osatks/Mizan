Mizan is a free Islamic app: Quran reader, prayer times, qibla, adhkar (supplications) and zakat/inheritance calculators. There is no login, no account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection, so no demo account is needed.

HOW TO TEST
1. First launch shows a short 5-page tour; tap "Skip" (or swipe to the end). The interface follows the device language (Arabic is the primary language; 15 languages in Settings > Language).
2. Location is optional. On Home, tap the location card: either allow "While Using" (used only on the device to compute prayer times and the qibla direction, never sent) or tap "Choose a city" and pick any city, e.g. Makkah. Denying location does not block anything.
3. Prayer tab: today's prayer times and iqama. Adhan alerts are optional local notifications scheduled on the device (enable them from the Prayer screen; the system asks for notification permission at that moment).
4. Quran tab: open any surah. Long-press an ayah for Tafsir, Listen, Save, Copy or Share.
5. Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio): long-press an ayah > Listen, then lock the device. The Quran recitation keeps playing on the lock screen. This is the only use of background audio.
6. Home > tools: Adhkar (tap a dhikr to count it; "Evidence" shows its hadith source and grade), Qibla compass (needs a device with a compass), Zakat, Inheritance, Hijri date, Tasbih.
7. Home Screen widgets: long-press the Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget > Mizan (next prayer, prayer times, adhkar, Hijri date, ayah of the day).

IPAD AND IPHONE DUO
This is a universal app with layouts designed for each screen size; please test on iPad too.
- iPad (all orientations, Split View, Slide Over and resizable windows): a sidebar replaces the bottom tab bar; Home shows the tools in 3-4 columns; Zakat, Inheritance and Prayer show inputs and results side by side; menus and sheets appear as centered popovers and windows.
- Quran on iPad in landscape: open a surah to see two facing pages like a printed Mushaf (odd page on the right). Long-press an ayah > Tafsir opens the tafsir in a side panel next to the pages. In portrait one page is shown at the printed page proportions. Rotating keeps the same page on screen.
- iPhone Duo: the outer display uses the phone layout; the inner display uses the two-page Quran and sidebar layouts. Folding or unfolding keeps the same page and any open panel.

NETWORK
The app works offline. It connects only to stream Quran recitation audio (everyayah.com) and, when the user asks, to download extra tafsirs (cdn.jsdelivr.net), language packs and adhan voices (mizan-al-muslem.com). No personal data is sent.

CONTENT
Quran text: Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), redistributed verbatim under its license. Tafsir al-Muyassar (King Fahd Complex) and al-Jalalayn; translations of the meanings from tanzil.net/trans. Recitations: EveryAyah.com. Sources are listed in Settings (About section).
