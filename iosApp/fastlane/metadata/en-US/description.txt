Mizan is a Muslim's companion for every day: the Holy Quran with tafsir and recitation, adhkar with their evidence, prayer times with adhan alerts, the qibla, and zakat and inheritance calculators, in one elegant app in 15 languages. Completely free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no account, and most of it works offline.

THE HOLY QURAN
• The 604 pages of the Madinah Mushaf in Uthmani script, or an ayah-by-ayah view with the translation of the meanings.
• Focus mode: the controls disappear, the screen stays on, and you turn the page by tapping its edge.
• A daily portion with khatma tracking and a reading streak; your place and bookmarks are saved.
• Long-press any ayah for tafsir, listen from it, bookmark, copy or share.

TAFSIR AND TRANSLATIONS
• Tafsir al-Muyassar and Tafsir al-Jalalayn are built in and work offline.
• A tafsir library you can download from when needed, and translations of the meanings in 15 languages.
• Tafsir opens in a panel above the page, and you can move to the next or previous ayah from there.

RECITATION AND MEMORIZATION
• Choose the reciter and the range: continuous, a page, a surah, or from one ayah to another.
• Repeat each ayah, pause after it, change the speed, or use the presets for listening, memorizing and review.
• Recitation keeps playing when the screen is locked.
• Memorization mode hides the ayahs so you can test yourself, then reveals them one by one with a tap.

ADHKAR WITH THEIR EVIDENCE
• 334 adhkar in 24 sections: morning and evening, after prayer, before sleep, Hisn al-Muslim and duas from the Quran.
• Every dhikr has an Evidence button: source, hadith number, grade and who graded it, narrator and virtue.
• A counter for each dhikr, and a digital tasbih with gentle haptics.

PRAYER, ADHAN AND QIBLA
• Accurate times for your city with all the major calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Egyptian Authority and more), and Hanafi Asr if you prefer.
• Iqama times after each adhan, and adhan alerts with the muezzin of your choice, or a quiet alert for each prayer.
• A qibla compass for your location, the Hijri date by the Umm al-Qura calendar, and upcoming Islamic events.
• Pick your city from the list, or use your location on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• Next prayer, today's prayer times, adhkar at their time, the Hijri date and the ayah of the day.

ZAKAT, INHERITANCE AND MORE
• A zakat calculator for the five kinds of wealth: money, gold, silver and shares; trade goods; crops; livestock; and buried treasure (rikaz).
• Zakat al-Fitr in money or food, fidya and kaffarah.
• An inheritance calculator following the science of fara'id: fixed shares, residuaries, exclusion, 'awl and radd, with the base of the case explained.
• Hijri date converter, age calculator and Islamic events.
• Every ruling comes with its evidence from the Quran, the Sunnah and the scholars.

LANGUAGES AND COLORS
• Arabic, English, French, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, Malay, Persian, Bengali, Russian, German, Spanish, Ukrainian, Uzbek and Kazakh.
• Ten app colors with dark mode, and one search bar that finds tools, cities, surahs, ayahs and adhkar.

PRIVACY FIRST
• We collect no data at all: no account, no tracking, no ads.
• Your location is used only on your device to compute prayer times and the qibla, and your settings stay with you.
• The internet is used only to play recitations and to download the tafsirs, languages and adhan voices you choose.

Results are a guide. For your own religious questions, refer to qualified scholars and official bodies.
