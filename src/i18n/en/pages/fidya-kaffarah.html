For those permanently unable to fast, such as the elderly and the sick with no hope of recovery: feeding one poor person for each day.

Number of days

For one who breaks an oath: feeding ten poor people, clothing them or freeing a slave; whoever cannot find the means must fast three days.

Number of kaffarahs (separate oaths)
If the oath about one and the same thing was repeated before expiation, only one kaffarah is due according to the majority of scholars.

In this order: freeing a slave; if not possible, fasting two consecutive months; if unable, feeding sixty poor people.
