Everything you need in your day

The Mushaf and focus mode

The 604 pages of the Madinah Mushaf in the Uthmani script, or a verse-by-verse view with translation of the meanings. In focus mode the buttons disappear and the screen stays on, and you turn the page by tapping its edge, with a daily wird and khatmah tracking.

Verse options with a long press

Touching the page does not interrupt your reading. Long-press any verse and a bar appears with: tafsir, listen from here, bookmark, copy and share.

Tafsir inside the page

Tafsir al-Muyassar and al-Jalalayn built in for offline use, a library of tafsirs downloaded when needed, and translations of the meanings in 15 languages. The tafsir opens in a panel over the page so the page does not move behind it, and you can move from it to the next and previous verse.

Recitation and its settings

Choose "Listen" to open the recitation settings from that very verse: the reciter, the range (continuous, page, surah, or from one verse to another), repetition of each verse, the pause after it and the speed, with ready-made presets for listening, memorisation and revision.

Memorisation assistant

In focus mode the verses are hidden so you can test your memorisation, then you reveal them verse by verse with a tap, or reveal the next one from the toolbar, with repeated recitation to help you memorise.

Adhkar with their evidence

334 adhkar in 24 sections from Hisn al-Muslim, morning and evening adhkar, prayer and sleep adhkar, and Quranic supplications. Every dhikr has an "Evidence" button: the source, hadith number, its grading and who graded it, the narrator and its virtue, with an interactive counter.

Prayer, adhan and Qibla

Accurate times for your city with all the adopted calculation methods, adhan alerts that work offline, a Qibla compass, and 5 home-screen widgets: prayer times, next prayer, verse of the day, Hijri date and khatmah.

Zakat, inheritance and more

A zakat calculator for the five types of wealth and Zakat al-Fitr, an inheritance calculator based on the science of fara'id with 'awl and radd, fidya and kaffarah, the Hijri calendar and events, and the tasbih, all with religious evidence.

15 languages and 10 colors

Arabic, English, French, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, Malay, Persian, Bengali, Russian, German, Spanish, Ukrainian, Uzbek and Kazakh, with new languages downloaded without updating the app. And ten interface colors with a night mode.
