Our mission

{{site_name}} is a complete Islamic technology project offering a range of free religious tools and daily acts of worship through the website and a smartphone app (available on Google Play, with an iPhone version coming soon), to help Muslims recite and reflect on the Book of Allah, keep up their adhkar and prayers, and organise their religious and financial affairs with ease and clarity. We started from a clear idea: a Muslim needs a reliable daily companion that combines religious authenticity with modern technology, without compromising on scholarly accuracy or user privacy.

We chose the name "Mizan" (the Balance) because it expresses the essence of what we strive for: justice, accuracy and balance in knowledge, action and calculation, as Allah the Exalted said: ﴿وَأَقِيمُوا الْوَزْنَ بِالْقِسْطِ وَلَا تُخْسِرُوا الْمِيزَانَ﴾ “And establish weight in justice and do not make deficient the balance” [Ar-Rahman: 9].

Our scholarly methodology and sources

We make sure that every tool and text in {{site_name}} rests on a documented scholarly and religious foundation:

1. The text of the Holy Quran and the tafsirs

For the text of the Holy Quran in the Uthmani script and the simplified text used for search, we rely on the internationally trusted Tanzil (Tanzil.net) project, and we use the Amiri Quran font, designed specifically for writing the Mushaf, with automated software tests that verify all verses of the Holy Quran (6236 verses) in their places letter by letter.

2. Fiqh and hadith sources

For religious rulings and adhkar we rely on the Holy Quran and the authentic Prophetic Sunnah from the two Sahihs and the recognised books of Sunan, stating the source and grading of every dhikr. In matters of juristic calculation (such as zakat, inheritance and fidya) we adopt the view of the majority of jurists and contemporary fiqh councils, and we attach a "Religious evidence" card to every screen, pointing out where valid scholarly differences exist and giving the user a choice wherever possible.

3. Astronomical calculations and the Hijri calendar

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated using precise astronomical and spherical equations based on the location's coordinates and the sun's angles, supporting the international calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Egyptian General Authority, and others). Hijri date conversion relies on the official Umm al-Qura calendar, noting that the start of the month may differ by one day depending on the religious sighting of the crescent moon.

Our values

  • Grounding and accuracy: we link every tool and ruling to its religious evidence from the Quran and Sunnah, and test the calculations against solved examples from books of fiqh and fara'id.
  • Complete privacy: all calculations and readings take place locally on your device, and the mobile app is entirely free of ads and any tracking software.
  • Free of charge and works offline: all our tools and apps are free for everyone, and the app works with its full Mushaf, both tafsirs, adhkar and calculators without an internet connection.
  • Clarity and education: we explain the calculation steps and the verses of rulings in every tool, so that Muslims understand how the religious result was derived.

How do we review content?

All religious content and every new tool in {{site_name}} goes through several stages before publication: collecting the rulings and texts from their original sources, then programming the tool and running comprehensive automated tests (for verses, adhkar, inheritance cases and language packs), followed by linguistic and technical review.
