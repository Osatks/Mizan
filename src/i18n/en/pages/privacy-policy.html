1. Our core principle: local processing on your device

All {{site_name}} tools and services — including the Holy Quran, the tafsirs and the adhkar, the zakat and Zakat al-Fitr calculators, the inheritance calculator, the Hijri date converter, the age calculator, prayer times, the Qibla direction, the digital tasbih, and the fidya and kaffarah calculator — are designed to run entirely locally on your device or within your browser. The figures, financial amounts, names of heirs, dates and your Quran and adhkar reading history are not sent to our servers; we do not store them and we never see them. Using the website or the app does not require creating an account or logging in.

2. Privacy of the smartphone app (Android & iOS — com.mizan.almuslim)

The {{site_name}} smartphone app adheres to the highest standards of privacy and security:

  • Completely free of ads and tracking: the mobile app contains no commercial advertisements and includes no analytics software or external tracking packages (Zero Ads & Zero Tracking SDKs).
  • We do not collect or share any personal data: the app does not collect, sell or share any personal or sensitive data with any third party, and nothing about you is sent to Mizan's servers. Only a few optional features send what they need directly to the services named below (such as an approximate location to find nearby mosques), without any identifier.
  • Works mostly offline: the Holy Quran in the Uthmani script, Tafsir al-Muyassar and Tafsir al-Jalalayn, the 334 adhkar, the two adhans bundled with the app, and all calculators, prayer times and the Qibla direction work without an internet connection. Audio recitations, additional tafsirs, language packs and additional adhan recordings need a connection the first time you request them.
  • Home-screen widgets: the widgets show upcoming prayer times, the Hijri date, adhkar and the ayah of the day. The app passes them this data only within your device (on iOS through the app’s private shared storage), and on iOS a widget may read your location on the device to calculate the times. None of this is sent to us.
  • Rating prompt: the app may occasionally show the official rating prompt from Apple (App Store) or Google Play. Apple or Google handles that prompt entirely, and Mizan receives no personal data from it.
  • iPhone search (Spotlight): on iPhone, the titles of surahs, ayahs, tools and adhkar are indexed in the iPhone's on-device search so you can find them from the Home Screen. This index stays on your device.

Mobile app permissions and how they are used

The mobile app may request the following optional permissions to run specific features on your device:

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION on Android, the “While Using the App” permission on iOS): an optional permission used on the device to calculate prayer times astronomically and determine the Qibla angle; on iOS the home-screen widgets may also use it on the same device to calculate the times. Your location is never sent to Mizan's servers or stored by us, and it leaves your device only for two optional uses described under the optional internet connection below: showing the name of your town and the nearby mosques tool. You can do without this permission by selecting your city manually from the built-in list of cities.
  • Notifications, alarms and adhan playback (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): used locally only to alert you when a prayer time begins, play the adhan you chose with a button to stop it, vibrate with the pattern you chose, and reschedule alerts automatically when the device restarts or the time zone changes. On iOS the alerts are local notifications with a short adhan sound bundled in the app.
  • Compass and motion sensor: read locally on the device to move the interactive Qibla compass towards the Holy Kaaba, without recording or sending any readings.

Optional internet connection within the app

The mobile app does not connect to the internet to collect data. It connects only for the following optional services when you request them, except for the content of your own language, which it downloads automatically in the background from the official website. The app attaches no personal data or identifiers to any of these connections:

  • Audio recitations of the Holy Quran: when you choose to listen to the recitation of verses, the app fetches the audio files for the requested verses and saves them in the cache on the device so you can listen to them later offline.
  • Library of additional tafsirs: when you wish to download additional works of tafsir (such as Tafsir Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari or al-Sa'di) to save and use on your device offline.
  • Additional language packs: when you open the language list and download or update a language pack and translation of the meanings of the Quran from the official website ({{site_url}}/lang/).
  • Translations, tafsirs, hadith and adhkar in other languages: downloaded on demand from the official website ({{site_url}}/content/) and saved on your device; the content for your own language downloads automatically in the background.
  • Additional adhan recordings: when you choose a muezzin that is not bundled with the app, the recording is downloaded from the official website ({{site_url}}/adhan/) and saved on your device.
  • Town name: when you use your current location, the phone's built-in geocoding service turns the coordinates into the name of your town: Apple's service on iPhone, and Google's system geocoder on Android.
  • Nearby mosques (optional tool): on iPhone, the search area around you and the search words are sent to Apple Maps (MapKit). On Android, your approximate location, rounded to about 100 metres, is sent over HTTPS to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de) without any identifiers; as with any web request, that server can see your IP address. The results are kept in memory only and are not saved, and opening directions to a mosque hands its destination to Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Your location is never sent to Mizan's servers, and we store none of it. You can avoid sending it anywhere by choosing your city manually and not using the nearby mosques tool.

4. Local storage on your device

We use local storage (localStorage in the browser, and SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults in the mobile app) to save your settings and progress locally on your device only, including:

  • The selected appearance (light or dark mode), your preferred interface color and display language.
  • The city, the astronomical calculation method and the juristic school for displaying prayer times, and the adhan alert settings.
  • Your last reading position in the Holy Quran, saved verses (bookmarks), khatmah tracking, daily reading streak, font size and selected tafsirs.
  • The number of tasbihs in the digital tasbih and your progress in the daily adhkar counters.

This data is never sent to us and stays on your device, apart from the backup and sync options below, which you control. You can delete it at any time by clearing the website data in your browser settings or clearing the app data in your phone's settings.

Backup and sync in the mobile app: you can save a backup file of your settings and progress; it stays wherever you save it. On iPhone, optional iCloud sync (off by default) syncs the same data through your own iCloud account using Apple's key-value storage, and we cannot see it. On Android, the system's automatic backup may back up the app's settings to your Google account, if you have it enabled.

5. Location in the browser

The prayer times and Qibla direction tools on the website optionally allow the use of your browser's location feature, after requesting your explicit permission. If you agree, the coordinates are used only on your device to calculate the times and the Qibla direction, and are not sent to or stored on our servers; you can refuse permission and choose your city manually.
