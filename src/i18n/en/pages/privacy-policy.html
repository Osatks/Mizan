1. Our core principle: local processing on your device

All {{site_name}} tools and services — including the Holy Quran, the tafsirs and the adhkar, the zakat and Zakat al-Fitr calculators, the inheritance calculator, the Hijri date converter, the age calculator, prayer times, the Qibla direction, the digital tasbih, and the fidya and kaffarah calculator — are designed to run entirely locally on your device or within your browser. The figures, financial amounts, names of heirs, dates and your Quran and adhkar reading history are not sent to our servers; we do not store them and we never see them. Using the website or the app does not require creating an account or logging in.

2. Privacy of the smartphone app (Android & iOS — com.mizan.almuslim)

The {{site_name}} smartphone app adheres to the highest standards of privacy and security:

  • Completely free of ads and tracking: the mobile app contains no commercial advertisements and includes no analytics software or external tracking packages (Zero Ads & Zero Tracking SDKs).
  • We do not collect or share any personal data: the app does not collect, transfer, sell or share any personal or sensitive data with any third party.
  • Works offline (Offline-First): the Holy Quran in the Uthmani script, Tafsir al-Muyassar and Tafsir al-Jalalayn, the 334 adhkar, and all calculators, prayer times and the Qibla direction work without needing an internet connection.

Mobile app permissions and how they are used

The mobile app may request the following optional permissions to run specific features locally on your device only:

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): an optional permission used exclusively on the device to calculate prayer times astronomically and determine the Qibla angle. Your location coordinates are not sent to any external server or saved outside your device, and you can do without this permission by selecting your city manually from the built-in list of cities.
  • Notifications and exact alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): used locally only to send prayer time and adhan alerts, and to reschedule alarms automatically when the device restarts or the time zone changes.
  • Compass and motion sensor: read locally on the device to move the interactive Qibla compass towards the Holy Kaaba, without recording or sending any readings.

Optional internet connection within the app

The mobile app does not connect to the internet automatically in the background to collect data; it only makes a direct connection when the user personally requests one of the following optional services:

  • Audio recitations of the Holy Quran: when you choose to listen to the recitation of verses, the app fetches the audio files for the requested verses and saves them in the cache on the device so you can listen to them later offline.
  • Library of additional tafsirs: when you wish to download additional works of tafsir (such as Tafsir Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari or al-Sa'di) to save and use on your device offline.
  • Additional language packs: when you open the language list and download or update a language pack and translation of the meanings of the Quran from the official website ({{site_url}}/lang/).

4. Local storage on your device

We use local storage (localStorage in the browser, and SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults in the mobile app) to save your settings and progress locally on your device only, including:

  • The selected appearance (light or dark mode), your preferred interface color and display language.
  • The city, the astronomical calculation method and the juristic school for displaying prayer times, and the adhan alert settings.
  • Your last reading position in the Holy Quran, saved verses (bookmarks), khatmah tracking, daily reading streak, font size and selected tafsirs.
  • The number of tasbihs in the digital tasbih and your progress in the daily adhkar counters.

This data stays on your device only and is not sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the website data in your browser settings or clearing the app data in your phone's settings.

If you agree, the coordinates are used only on your device to calculate the times and the Qibla direction, and are not sent to or stored on our servers; you can refuse permission and choose your city manually.
