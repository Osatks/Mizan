Adhan recordings used by Mizan

Every recording below was published by its muezzin or by the person who recorded it, under a licence that
allows redistribution (CC0, the Public Domain Mark, CC BY or CC BY-SA). Mizan's versions are derived works:
cut to the adhan itself, converted to mono, high-pass filtered at 80 Hz, loudness-normalised (-16 LUFS, EBU R128
measurement, peaks limited to -1.5 dB) with short fades, and encoded as AAC 64 kbps (.m4a, full adhan) and as a 28-second
opening excerpt with a fade-out (IMA4 .caf, iPhone notification sound). The processing script is
android-app/tools/make_adhan.py. The derived files are offered under the same terms as their sources: the
CC BY recordings with the attribution given below, and the CC BY-SA recordings under CC BY-SA (that licence
applies to those audio files only, not to the rest of the app).

Recordings by famous muezzins of the Two Holy Mosques, Mishary Alafasy, Abdul Basit and other well-known
studio or broadcast recordings are not included: no copy with a licence that allows redistribution was found.
Recordings captured from radio or television broadcasts were rejected even when the uploader marked them as
public domain.
